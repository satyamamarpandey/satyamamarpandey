I am Satyam Pandey, a quantitative analyst, machine learning practitioner, software engineer and product builder.

Professionally, I work as a Senior Quantitative Analyst at Exelon, where I work across quantitative analytics, forecasting, machine learning, customer intelligence, large-scale data systems and decision-support workflows.

🧭 My Story

I did not begin by building AI agents.

I started by building software, then moved deeper into cloud systems, data science, machine learning, quantitative analytics and eventually autonomous software.

2018 | Software Engineering

I worked as a Software Engineer at The Tech Inc in Nagpur, India.

I worked on customer-facing software, web development, user experience, search visibility and product improvements.

That was where I learned one of the most useful lessons in engineering:

A technically correct product still has to solve a problem people care about.

2019 to 2022 | Cloud, Analytics and Enterprise Engineering

I joined ZS Associates as a Cloud Application Engineer and Developer.

My work expanded into:

Cloud infrastructure

Software engineering

Analytics applications

Data visualization

AWS

Cost analysis

Performance optimization

Enterprise software systems

I received Great Employee recognition during my time at ZS Associates.

2022 to 2024 | Graduate School, Research and Machine Learning

I moved to the United States to pursue a Master of Science in Computer Science at NJIT.

I graduated with a 3.90 / 4.00 GPA and received an Academic Scholarship.

During this period, I worked as a Data Scientist and Machine Learning Engineer with NJIT / NSF, applying machine learning to pharmaceutical and scientific problems.

This work pushed me deeper into predictive modeling, research, experimentation and building software around analytical models.

2024 to Present | Quantitative Analytics at Exelon

I joined Exelon as a Senior Quantitative Analyst.

My work now spans:

Quantitative modeling

Predictive analytics

Customer intelligence

Journey analytics

Forecasting

Large-scale SQL and Spark workflows

Data engineering

Decision-support systems

Power BI

Cloud analytical environments

Automation

Selected prior work includes improving energy affordability forecasting by approximately 7% and contributing to improvements in strategic scenario analysis.

Today | Building Beyond My Day Job

Today I work professionally in quantitative analytics while independently building software across several problem spaces under Brandsap.

I am interested in the point where:

AI + software engineering + data + infrastructure + measurement = systems that can perform useful work

That is what I keep trying to build.
